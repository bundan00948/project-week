Important Service Update / 重要服務公告
English

Dear Members, we want to inform you that Macau Traffic Monitor has paused its real-time traffic data and community chat services. Because this is an independent project, the monthly database and server expenses have become too high for us to pay out of our own pockets. To stop these growing costs immediately, we have turned off the live database system and put it into hibernation mode.

We are thinking about coming back by December to restart a simpler version of the website, but this depends on finding cheaper ways to run the service. Honestly, a return is currently unlikely. For now, your account profiles and settings are saved securely in our offline backup, and no user data will be removed until our final decision. Thank you so much for using our app, sharing road updates, and helping each other avoid traffic jams in Macau. Please use the official DSAT platforms for your daily travel updates.

繁體中文

各位會員好：我們很抱歉地通知大家，澳門交通監察（Macau Traffic Monitor）已暫停即時路況數據和社區聊天室服務。由於這是一個獨立運作的項目，維持服務器和數據庫的每月開銷越來越高，我們已經無法繼續自費承擔。為了立即停止這筆持續產生的費用，我們目前已關閉即時數據庫系統，並將其轉入休眠狀態。

我們目前正考慮是否在 12 月底前，以更簡單、省錢的方式重新上線，但這完全取決於能否找到更便宜的運行方案。坦白說，目前重新回歸的可能性較低。請放心，您的賬戶資料和設定均已安全地保存在離線備份中，沒有任何數據丟失知道十二月的最終決定。非常感謝大家一直以來使用我們的應用程式、分享即時路況，並互相幫助避開澳門的塞車黑點。在服務暫停期間，請使用官方的交通事務局（DSAT）平台獲取每日最新路況。

With gratitude / 衷心感謝，
Brendan Liu Head Developer / 澳門交通監察團隊
